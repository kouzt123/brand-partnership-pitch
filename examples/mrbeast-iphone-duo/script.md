# Everybody in the photo

**Unofficial concept ad.** Not commissioned, approved or endorsed by MrBeast or Apple. All dialogue, prizes and participants are fictional creative proposals.

40 seconds · 9:16 · Six illustrated scenes

### 00–06s · The offer

**Visual:** Wide vertical shot. MrBeast stands closest to camera with six adult contestants arranged behind him. He gestures toward the group; a closed phone is in his other hand.

> MRBEAST: All seven of us in one photo, and you six split ten grand.

**On-screen text:** 7 PEOPLE / 1 PHOTO / $10,000

**Direction:** A fictional giveaway setup. Leave a beat for the group reaction.

### 06–12s · The catch

**Visual:** Medium shot from beside the group. MrBeast lifts the closed phone to compose a photo; a contestant points from him toward the empty spot in the lineup.

> CONTESTANT: Wait. You’re not in it.
> MRBEAST: And nobody gets to hold the camera.

**On-screen text:** WHO TAKES THE PHOTO?

**Direction:** Use a quick reaction cut. The phone is still folded; no tripod.

### 12–19s · The product move

**Visual:** Close insert. Hands open iPhone Duo and set it on a waist-high plinth in a stable self-supporting folded pose, camera oriented toward the group.

> MRBEAST [VO]: iPhone Duo. Fold it, set it down. Smart Take handles the photo.

**On-screen text:** iPhone Duo / Smart Take

**Direction:** Show the actual supported camera setup. UI is schematic in these planning drawings.

### 19–27s · Get everyone in

**Visual:** Wide from just behind the phone. MrBeast steps into the center of the six-person group, turns toward the camera and settles into a pose.

> MRBEAST: It takes the shot when everyone’s ready. Even me.
> CONTESTANT: Get in here!

**On-screen text:** EVERYONE IN

**Direction:** Allow the pose and capture to happen; do not promise an exact capture latency.

### 27–34s · The payoff

**Visual:** Medium reaction. The group leans toward the opened phone in MrBeast’s hands. One contestant points at the captured group photo; he reacts with an open-handed gesture.

> CONTESTANT: We got everybody!
> MRBEAST: That’s ten grand. Split it six ways.
> [Group cheers.]

**On-screen text:** EVERYONE MADE IT

**Direction:** The photo and payout are proposed story beats, not documentary footage or a real offer.

### 34–40s · The close

**Visual:** Medium hero shot. MrBeast faces the viewer with the opened phone at chest height. The group celebrates softly behind him; the final product and concept labels sit outside the artwork.

> MRBEAST: Nobody stuck behind the camera. Explore iPhone Duo.

**On-screen text:** iPhone Duo / Explore at apple.com/iphone-duo

**Direction:** End card retains “Unofficial concept ad” outside the sketch. No claim of sponsorship.
