## Original-image reference inputs

The host's face was referenced from the Gemini video at 29.15s and the car video at 1.00s. Each of the five host-containing storyboard calls included both source frames. Scene 3 is a product-only insert. Public source context identifies the creator; no bystander was identified from a face. The other six adults are newly generated fictional characters.

[Apple's iPhone Duo page](https://www.apple.com/iphone-duo/) supplied the product information and images for the rear design, opened form and supported standing pose. The recommended concept uses foldable placement and Smart Take; alternatives use Split View and Duo FaceTime. [Apple's announcement](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/09/apple-unveils-iphone-duo/) distinguishes announcement from later availability. The script avoids a purchase-now claim and unverified personal testimony.
